# 08 · Data Center (backend)
### The documentalist's home — where quality starts before the LLM

> Inherits FOUNDATION + File 01. This is where the Documentalist — the RFP's central role — lives. It makes the 7-stage pipeline and the metadata contract *operable by internal teams*, which is the self-sufficiency the RFP scores. The documentalist is a **criteria editor**, not a file clerk.

---

## 1. The job

Manage the sources feeding the SSoT, watch documents flow through ingestion, and — the core loop — **validate the metadata and classification the system proposes**. The system prepares; the person decides. Every validation makes the Wiki smarter.

---

## 2. Three areas

- **Sources & connectors** — what's connected, health, cadence.
- **Ingestion** — the live pipeline view: what's flowing, what's quarantined.
- **Validation queue** — the documentalist's daily work: approve/correct proposed metadata + classification, resolve conflicts.

## 5. The validation queue (the heart of this page)

```
┌─ Awaiting your review (3) ────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  Results_Q1_DE.pptx                                                   │
│  system proposes:  market DE · brand Telefónica · axis Innovation ●   │
│                    confidentiality confidential · owner Finance DE     │
│                    validity valid→Q2 · v1.2                           │
│  confidence 0.91   [ ✓ approve ]  [ edit ]  [ reject ]                │
│  ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────      │
│  ⚠ CONFLICT · Spain SoV                                                │
│  old deck says 31% · Talkwalker says 34% →                            │
│  [ keep 34% (fresh) · retain 31% as historic ]  → compiles to Wiki    │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **System proposes, person validates.** The AI fills all **3 taxonomy layers** — deterministic (what it is), semantic (what it says), strategic (which axis) — and the documentalist approves in one click or corrects. High-confidence auto-approves; medium goes here; low is flagged unlabeled.
- **Every correction trains the system** — it becomes a compiled fact in the Wiki, and improves future classification. The screen should say so quietly ("this correction refined the German position page").
- **Conflict resolution** — where two valid sources disagree, the documentalist fixes the "version of truth" and marks the loser historic. *This is the compounding moat, operated here and surfaced on the Wiki.*

---

## 6. Governance & taxonomy-as-configuration

- A governance panel edits the taxonomy / axes; a change triggers **re-classification, not re-embedding** (the investment-protection story) — 4 steps: edit → mapping table → assisted re-classify → human validation. No IT, no redeploy.
- Every action is logged (who uploaded/modified/validated) → audit-ready, satisfying Clause VI.
- SLA/freshness per document: last check, validity, "review > 6 months" nudges.

---

## 7. States

- **Nothing to review** → "You're all caught up — 96% of sources validated." Calm, not empty.
- **Quarantine backlog** → surfaced as the primary task, since unvalidated data is the enemy of trust.
- **Low-confidence classification** → clearly flagged as needing a human, never silently guessed.

---

## 8. Why this page matters

It's the RFP's whole operating model made real: the documentalist as criteria editor, metadata AI-assisted-but-human-validated, taxonomy editable without IT, everything audited. And it's the engine of the moat — **this is where the knowledge becomes a compounding asset instead of a static pile.** Copilot has no equivalent because it has no one governing the truth.
