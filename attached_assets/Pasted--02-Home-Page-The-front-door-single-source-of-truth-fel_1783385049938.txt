# 02 · Home Page
### The front door — "single source of truth," felt in three seconds

> Inherits everything in File 01. The Home's job: communicate **trust and readiness instantly**, route to the four apps, and surface **what's alive** — without becoming a dashboard. Calm, not busy.

---

## 1. What the Home must accomplish (in priority order)

1. **Establish trust on sight** — this is *the* source of truth; it should feel governed and current before you read a word.
2. **Be the front door to knowledge** — the ask bar is the primary action; most sessions start with a question.
3. **Route to the four apps** — Generate, KPIs, Planning, and the Chat/Agent.
4. **Show what changed** — a live radar: alerts, freshly validated knowledge, items awaiting *your* review.
5. **Adapt to the role** — Director vs Documentalist see different emphasis.

What it must **not** do: become a metrics wall, bury the ask bar, or show anything the user has no permission to see.

## 3. The ask bar (the front door)

- **The single most prominent element.** Large, centered, calm. The hero *is* the ask bar — not a marketing headline, not a big number. (Per design discipline: the hero is a thesis, and this product's thesis is "ask, and get a defensible answer.")
- Placeholder in the user's language. Rotating example prompts drawn from real demo beats ("revenue we defend for Q1 2026", "messaging doc tying Q1 results to Transform & Grow", "why is sentiment falling in Brazil this week?") — these double as a **guided tour** of what the product can do.
- Submitting opens **Ask** (File: chat) with the answer already resolving — the Home flows *into* the product, no dead click.
- Below the bar, one quiet line: *"Answers cite their source. If there's no evidence, I'll say so."* — sets the honesty contract on the front door.

---

## 4. The four app cards

Each card is a **live preview**, not a static tile — it shows the one number that makes you want to open it:

| Card | Live preview shows | Opens to |
|---|---|---|
| **Generate** | "Talking points · Press · Documents" + last draft in progress | the generation engine / Canvas |
| **KPIs** | "12 tracked · 2 off-target" (color-coded) | the KPI panel |
| **Planning** | "3 events this week · 1 conflict ⚠" | the calendar |
| **Ask / Agent** | "Chat with all your knowledge" | full Ask view |

Cards are permission-aware (counts reflect only what you can see) and use the shared vocabulary — axis pills, the amber for off-target, the warning glyph for conflicts. No card is louder than the ask bar.

---

## 5. The Radar — what's changed

A short, scannable feed (max ~5 items) of **things that moved**, each with a timestamp and, where relevant, an Evidence Chip:
- **External signals** — mention spikes, sentiment shifts, competitor moves (from the social/press layer).
- **Knowledge events** — "X validated by Y," "conflict resolved," "new source ingested."
- **Your queue** — "N documents awaiting your metadata review →" (documentalists) or "N drafts awaiting approval" (editors).

Every radar item is a **doorway**, not a notification — clicking goes straight to the thing. This is where the product feels *alive and governed at once*.

---

## 6. The knowledge-health strip (the quiet trust signal)

A single restrained line at the base of the home:
> **1,240 sources · 96% validated · last updated 11 min ago · ES · EN · DE · PT**

This does enormous work: it silently proves the corpus is *current, governed, multilingual, and mostly human-validated* — the exact things the SharePoint+Copilot incumbent can't claim. It's the governance moat, stated as a status line, not a pitch. Tapping it opens the Data Center.

---

## 7. Role-adaptive home

Same skeleton, re-weighted by role (from Entra ID):
- **Director** → radar + committee-prep prompt suggestions lead; validation queue hidden.
- **Documentalist** → the validation queue and ingestion health rise to the top; a "3 awaiting review" becomes the primary call to action.
- **Brand manager** → Brand Room activity + guardian flags surface.

---

## 8. States

- **First run / empty** — no metrics theater. A calm invitation: the ask bar, plus "Connect SharePoint or upload your first document to begin building your source of truth." The four cards show as available-but-empty with a one-line "what this becomes."
- **Loading** — the shell renders instantly; radar and health stream in progressively (never block the ask bar on data).
- **Restricted** — a user with narrow permissions simply sees fewer sources/cards; nothing says "denied" on the home — locks live where the content lives, not here.

---

## 9. Why this home wins the demo

In the first three seconds, before anyone asks a question, the evaluator sees: a governed, multilingual, freshly-updated source of truth with a calm ask bar promising cited answers and honest "no evidence." That *is* the pitch — the Home makes the differentiator felt before the first click. Then the ask bar carries them straight into the €8,127M Q1 2026 defensible-figure moment.
