# 05 · Generate  (Category B · Document generation)
### One engine, three shapes — brief in, on-brand cited draft out, edited on the Canvas

> Inherits FOUNDATION + File 01. The second hero. **Messaging documents (argumentarios)**, **Press releases & Q&A**, and **Multi-format documents** are the *same engine* wearing three skins (Cases 2, 3, 4). The **Canvas** is the shared editing surface. Everything generated is cited, on-brand, versioned, and human-gated.

---

## 1. The job

A Comms/Brand user asks for a committee messaging doc, a press release, a Q&A, or any corporate document — and the platform composes a **first draft respecting brand, tone, and regulation, every claim cited**, in ~90 seconds. The user *edits*, they don't investigate. Then exports on-brand and saves a versioned copy.

---

## 2. The engine, three shapes

| Shape (Case) | Adds over the base engine | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **Messaging docs (2)** | audience toggle (internal/external); umbrella → key messages by axis → cited proof → close; **spokesperson notes** | `.docx/.pptx/.pdf` |
| **Press & Q&A (3)** | subtype (release/Q&A); boilerplate + approved-quotes library; per-answer citation; internal-notes flag (non-exportable) | release `.docx/.pdf`; Q&A `.docx` |
| **Multi-format (4)** | template picker (or NL→suggestion); **chart-from-data**; **scheduled mode** | any doc `.docx/.pptx/.pdf` |

Build the base engine once; the shapes are configuration + template.

---

## 3. The flow (guided, with a chat rail)

```
BRIEF ─────────► DRAFTING ───────────► CANVAS ──────► EXPORT
pick shape      retrieve→outline→      edit by chat   .docx/.pptx/.pdf
+ params        Brand Guardian→charts→  + inline;      on-brand + save
(chat or form)  compose (cited) ~90s    every claim    versioned (3-layer)
                watchable, real steps   stays cited
```

**Brief (light).** Short form *or* natural language: topic · axis(es) · audience · language · destination confidentiality · format. "Topic" absorbs product/campaign. A director starts a deck in two lines — no wizard wall.

**Drafting (watchable, not theater).** Show the *real* pipeline running — "retrieving evidence → outlining → Brand Guardian checking tone → generating charts → composing." It's genuinely multi-step; proving it's real is the point. ~90s for a 10-slide deck.

---

## 4. The hard confidentiality gate (internal vs external)

This is a scored governance moment — make it visible:

- **Internal audience** → may retrieve **private/confidential** per the user's permissions (e.g. Internal Comms with confidential access handles Q1 figures *before* official publication).
- **External audience** → retrieves **only public/approved**. Off-the-record and confidential chunks are **structurally excluded** — they can't reach an external output. (The Venezuela "do not confirm timelines" note can inform an internal Q&A but **never** appears in a public release.)

The gate is enforced at retrieval, not by asking the model to behave.

## 5. The Canvas (the shared surface)

```
┌──────────────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────┐
│  DRAFT (on-brand, editable)       │  REFINE                            │
│  Message 1 · Ambitious management │  chat: "add the net-debt figure"   │
│  "We closed Q1 with €8,127M in    │        "soften the Defence point"  │
│   revenue, improved profitability │        "add a chart for accesses"  │
│   and cash; we confirm 2026        │  ── or edit inline ──              │
│   targets."                       │                                    │
│  [ €8,127M · Results_Q1 › s4 ·    │  SPOKESPERSON NOTES                │
│    vFinal · IR · ✓ ]             │  • "when will consolidation        │
│                                   │     happen?" → market first,        │
│  · chart: accesses (generated,    │     then Europe                     │
│    cited)         Ambitious mgmt ●│  • what NOT to say: no specific    │
│                                   │     deal timelines                  │
│  [ Message ◂ 1/3 ▸ ]             │  ASSETS (governed): templates ·    │
│                                   │  approved claims · disclaimers ·    │
│                                   │  glossary · brand book              │
├──────────────────────────────────┴────────────────────────────────────┤
│  Brand Guardian ✓ on-brand · 1 claim needs a source · export ▾ · save   │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Left: the draft**, on-brand (real corporate template), every claim an Evidence Chip, charts generated *from retrieved data* (not pasted) and cited, axis pills per section.
- **Right: refine** — a conversational window ("swap Q4→Q1", "tighten for committee") **and** direct inline editing.
- **Spokesperson notes** (internal messaging docs) — anticipates tough questions **and an explicit "what not to say"** (e.g. don't anticipate specific deal timelines; dividend nuances). This is defensibility built into the artifact.
- **Assets on the platform** — templates, approved claims, disclaimers, glossary, tone — already governed and versioned; the user brings a brief, not the assets.
- **Brand Guardian bar** — live status; **blocks export** until every claim is cited and no unapproved claim ("European leader") slips through, proposing a cited, dated, disclaimered rewrite instead.

## 6. Real anchored examples (for the build)

**Case 2 — internal messaging doc, Q1 2026 results tied to Transform & Grow.**
Topic = "Q1 2026 results + T&G narrative" · axes = [Ambitious management, European consolidation, Innovation & competitiveness] · ES · confidential. Retrieves the Q1 deck (€8,127M, 2026 targets, dividend €0.15), the T&G plan (€18–22bn synergies, 4 markets), prior approved messaging, KPIs (share +5.7%). Composes umbrella + key messages by axis, each with its cited figure; spokesperson notes on "when will consolidation happen?" and what not to anticipate.

**Case 3 — Chile sale press release + Q&A.**
Event = "Chile subsidiary sale" (10 Feb 2026, NJJ + Millicom, up to $1,365M) · external · public. Release: headline · lead · body with cited figure · executive quote · boilerplate · press contact — all public, on-brand. Q&A anticipates "why exit Spanish America?", "what amount/who buys?", "what remains?" (Venezuela — **internal note, do not confirm timelines**), "how does it fit T&G?". Distribution stays in Telefónica's PR tool (out of scope).

**Case 4 — scheduled weekly reputation report** (see §7).

## 7. Scheduled mode (Case 4 — the automation loop)

For recurring documents (weekly activity/reputation summary, 10-day forecast, half-year results):
- An **Admin** defines a scheduled document: template · sources/queries · frequency (e.g. Monday 08:00) · language · owner · **review folder**.
- On trigger, the agent generates on-brand with fresh cited data and **charts-from-data** — but **nothing auto-publishes**. It lands in the owner's review folder; they're notified (Teams/email).
- Owner opens it in the Canvas, adjusts, **approves** → then it exports/saves. Human gate always. *(This is the only place scheduled automation lives — not in Ask.)*

---

## 8. Export & versioning

Export `.docx/.pptx/.pdf` on the real Telefónica corporate templates. **Save versioned** into the SSoT with full **3-layer tags** → the output becomes governed E-data, traceable and reusable. Editorial/documentalist review before anything sensitive publishes.

---

## 9. States & restraint

- **Missing brief params** → one smart question, not a form wall.
- **No evidence for a requested claim** → Guardian flags it in the draft ("no approved source"), never invents.
- **Empty** → "Start from a template, or describe the document you need."
- Chips collapse to a dot; the draft never looks like a citation swamp. Remove one accessory.

---

## 10. Why this page wins

It turns days of committee-deck prep into 90 seconds — but it beats a generic AI writer for the same reason Ask beats Copilot: **every figure is defensible, every output on-brand by construction, external outputs can't leak confidential data, and nothing publishes without a human.** Generate is where "speed" and "trust" stop being a trade-off.
