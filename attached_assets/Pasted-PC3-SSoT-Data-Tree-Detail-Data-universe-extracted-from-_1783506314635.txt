PC3 · SSoT Data Tree (Detail)
Data universe extracted from the RFP. It is used so that suppliers can size connectors and parsers and generate
representative synthetic data by category (we do not expose real Telefónica data in the proposal — closed circuit).

│ ├── PDF (sector reports, legislation, news)

│ └── Initial dump of current SharePoint SSoT (no maximum number of documents)

│ ├── Brand
│ ├── Legal entity
│ ├── Year / quarter

│ ├── Confidentiality level (public · private · confidential)
│ ├── Owner
│ └── Validity / expiry (+ status / version)
│
├── E · DATA GENERATED AND MANAGED BY THE SSoT (versioned outputs)
│ ├── Talking points · press releases · Q&A · dossiers · reports (Word/PPT/charts, on-brand)
│ ├── KPIs / objective tracking
│ └── Calendars / planning (incl. 10-day forecasts)
│
└── CROSS-CUTTING

the RFI PoC outputs as a template (marketing report, compliance check). Optional: Telefónica provides anonymised
